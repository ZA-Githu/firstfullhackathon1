## Building Your AI-Native Team and Culture

The senior-to-junior ratio shifts because AI amplifies senior judgment more than junior output.
A senior engineer who can evaluate AI architecture proposals is five times more productive.
The dedicated QA role dissolves as everyone owns quality through evaluation frameworks.

### Hiring for AI-Native Mindset

Evaluative judgment is the most critical hiring criterion. Give candidates AI-generated output and
ask them to find the issues in fifteen minutes. Also assess prompt craftsmanship, comfort with
non-determinism, and documentation-first mindset. Hire for curiosity first. Curious people update
their mental models as technology evolves.

### Culture Norms and The Long Game

Core culture norms: specs before code always with no exceptions, significant decisions require
Architecture Decision Records, prompt history records log every important AI interaction, and
living documents are updated when requirements change.

Track throughput alongside quality. Measure features shipped per engineer per month and production
incident rate together. The teams that win long-term built the culture to continuously adapt as
models and tools evolve. Build with AI. Evaluate ruthlessly. Document everything. Adapt continuously.
